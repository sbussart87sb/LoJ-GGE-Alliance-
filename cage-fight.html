🛡️ Standardized Troop Ratios & Directives

To maximize damage scaling and ensure frontline integrity during alliance-wide cage battles, all participating squads are configured to the optimized standard layout:

  • Shields: 0% (Strictly Prohibited)
  • Grenadiers / Bombers: 20,000 troops (20%)
  • Shooters: 80,000 troops (80%)
⚠️ MANDATORY DIRECTIVE: Never run Shields during the Cage Fight event! Keep lines open and active.

📋 Solo Rally Guidelines

When initiating or commanding your own independent rallies outside of primary alliance coordination blocks, commanders are requested to strictly maintain the identical 0% Shields / 20% Grenadiers / 80% Shooters configuration to preserve damage output consistency.
