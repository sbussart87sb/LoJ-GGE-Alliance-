Cage Fight Strategy

Server 199 • Tactical Arena Breakdown

🏟️ Event Overview: Cage Fight

The Cage Fight event demands precise squad deployment and aggressive tactical choices. Understanding when to engage head-on or bypass commanders is the key to securing higher rankings.

🦸 Recommended Heroes

  • Primary Combat Heroes: Deploy high-burst damage and defense-scaling commanders to hold the ground inside the arena.
  • Synergy Focus: Match skills that boost troop defense and counter enemy frontline engagements.

⚙️ Tactics: Heroes vs. Troop Rush

  • Why Use Heroes: Best utilized when facing sustained brawls where commander skills can turn the tide through area-of-effect damage and survivability buffs.
  • Why Go No Heroes / Troop Rush: Effective for maximizing raw deployment speed and overwhelming opponents purely through high-volume numerical advantages before enemy skills can cycle.
