⚔️ Cage Fights Meta & Best Selections

Cage Fights demand isolation tactics, self-sustain, and high burst potential rather than traditional army scaling. Below are the top recommendations for dominating the cage:

  • Top Tier Pick (High Self-Sustain & Stun): Choose heroes who feature built-in lifesteal or crowd-control locks. Reason: In a 1v1 enclosed cage environment, healing through damage or locking down the opponent's active skill prevents them from executing their burst combo.
  • Alternative Counter Pick (Pure Burst): High-tier Season 6 Shooters. Reason: If you can eliminate the opponent before they spin up their defensive cycles, raw execution speed wins the match instantly.
  • Avoid: Pure defensive shield heroes with low attack scaling. Reason: Cage fights have time limits or shrinkage mechanics where passive tanking usually results in a timeout loss.
