Terminal Siege Strategy

Server 199 • Coordinated Defense & Scoring

🚨 Event Overview: Terminal Siege

Terminal Siege is a cooperative wave-defense event where waves of enemies attack alliance member bases. Success relies heavily on collective alliance alignment rather than isolated individual defense.

⚠️ Crucial Rule: Turn Off Prison Self-Defense

You must turn off self-defense on your own prison for this event. Working as a unified group allows the alliance to pool forces and strategy effectively. Trying to fight solo or keeping individual defense systems active fragments your potential points—coordinating and working as a group unlocks significantly higher score rewards for everyone.

🛡️ Tactical Execution & Tips

  • Group Rallying: Coordinate reinforcement deployments with alliance members to secure vulnerable perimeters.
  • Maximize Point Yields: Share defensive burdens so that high-damage and high-scoring waves are handled collectively, multiplying total alliance output.
