Terminal Siege Strategy

Server 199 • Defensive Grid & Wave Breakdown

🛡️ Event Overview: Terminal Siege

Terminal Siege requires high-level coordination, solid defensive positioning, and structured team wave-clear tactics to hold back heavy enemy pressure and secure max milestone rewards.

⚡ Tactical Deployment & Strategy

  • Base Defense Protocol (Mandatory): You must make sure to turn OFF "Defend Base" (enable "Do Not Defend Base") during the event. Defending individually is selfish and hurts the team's total points; turning it off lets the team gather points together properly.
  • Lane Holdings: Coordinate your squad placements carefully to block incoming enemy pressure points and prevent leaks through weak flanks.
  • Hero Roster Integration: Make use of high-utility damage-dealing and crowd-control heroes on-station for maximum area-of-effect damage efficiency.
  • Wave Management: Conserve major ultimate skills and heavy cooldown rotations for elite waves rather than wasting them on early mob spawns.
  • Alliance Reinforcements: Keep communication channels open with R4 and alliance leadership to shift reinforcements immediately where lane pressure spikes.
⚠️ CRITICAL PROTOCOL: Do not break formation during mid-wave spikes, and ensure "Do Not Defend Base" is active so the entire alliance maximizes point scaling!
